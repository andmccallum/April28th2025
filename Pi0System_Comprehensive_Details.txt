Description: Optimizes resource allocation across the system
Equation: $$R_{opt} = \arg\min_{R} \sum_{i=1}^{n} C_i(R_i) \text{ subject to } \sum_{i=1}^{n} R_i \leq R_{total}$$

- correlation_handling: Full covariance matrix
